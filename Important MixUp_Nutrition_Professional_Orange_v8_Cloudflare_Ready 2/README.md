# MixUp Nutrition — Professional v7

Cloudflare Pages-ready static website.

## Design direction
Built from public structural patterns observed on Azelis and Barentz: clear market segmentation, ingredient categories, application/technical support, manufacturer partnerships, and strong contact pathways. It is an original MixUp Nutrition design and does not copy their branding, text, logos or proprietary assets.

## Edit
Open `content.js` to change company details, image URLs, market cards, product categories and capabilities. No build process is required. Upload the entire folder to Cloudflare Pages.

## Pages
Home, About, Markets, Capabilities, Products, Manufacturers, Contact.
